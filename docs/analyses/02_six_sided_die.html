1 Research Question

How quickly does averaging a discrete uniform die produce a sampling distribution that is comfortable to call approximately normal?

2 Population / Process and Theory

X_i is uniform on {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6}. The theoretical mean is 3.5, variance is 35/12, and the theoretical SE is sqrt(35/12) / sqrt(n).

3 CLT Assumptions and Prediction

The draws are iid with finite variance, so the ordinary CLT applies. Symmetry should reduce skewness, while discreteness may remain visible at small n.

4 Simulation Design, Search, and Evidence

The runner uses B = 10,000, a broad grid, and optional refinements around a reviewed transition. The display uses the largest generated n only as a reproducible diagnostic view; it is not a selected N.
