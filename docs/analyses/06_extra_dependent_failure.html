1 Research Question

How does the sampling distribution of a mean change when each operating time depends on the previous operating time?

2 Population / Process and Theory

The instructor model sets the next failure rate to baseline_rate * exp(beta * (previous_time - reference_time)), then draws the next operating time conditionally from an Exponential distribution. The supplied values are baseline rate 1, beta = 1.25, and reference time 1. A long operating period tends to be followed by a shorter one, creating compensating dependence.

3 CLT Assumptions and Prediction

Observations are not independent and are not one fixed iid exponential distribution. The variance of a mean includes covariance terms, so the iid sigma / sqrt(n) formula is not used as if it were valid.

6 Final Selected N and Interpretation

Code 
report_selection(spec$id)

Selected N: No finite defensible value identified within the tested range.

Evidence decision: No defensible n was identified through n = 1,000; dependence and persistent positive skew make the ordinary iid SE shortcut inappropriate.

Through n = 1,000, the process improves but still has positive skewness and Q-Q error above the Normal calibration. The N/A is therefore a tested-range decision, not a claim that no dependent-process CLT could ever be proved. The lag plot and negative lag-1 association also show why an iid standard-error shortcut is unsafe.
