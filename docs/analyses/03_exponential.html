1 Research Question

How much averaging is needed to overcome the strong right skew of an Exponential(rate = 1) population?

2 Population / Process and Theory

X_i ~ Exponential(rate = 1), with mean 1, standard deviation 1, and theoretical SE 1 / sqrt(n).

3 CLT Assumptions and Prediction

The observations are iid and have finite variance, so the ordinary CLT applies. I expect the right tail and positive skewness to be visible for small n, with empirical SE approaching 1 / sqrt(n) as n grows.

4 Simulation Design, Search, and Evidence

Use B = 10,000. Inspect the broad grid first, then enter a transition center in R/config.R to add nearby values.

## Research Question

How much averaging is needed to overcome the strong right skew of an Exponential(rate = 1) population?

## Population / Process and Theory

`X_i ~ Exponential(rate = 1)`, with mean `1`, standard deviation `1`, and theoretical SE `1 / sqrt(n)`.

## CLT Assumptions and Prediction

The observations are iid and have finite variance, so the ordinary CLT applies. I expect the right tail and positive skewness to be visible for small `n`, with empirical SE approaching `1 / sqrt(n)` as `n` grows.

## Simulation Design, Search, and Evidence

Use `B = 10,000`. Inspect the broad grid first, then enter a transition center in `R/config.R` to add nearby values.
